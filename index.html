About the Fellowship

Jerome Hill Artist Fellowships support early career generative artists and culture bearers in Minnesota and the five boroughs of New York City who take creative risks, express a clear creative vision, commit deeply to their craft, and engage responsibly with audiences and communities.

Artists Who Take Creative Risks

  • Exploring
  • Expanding
  • Re-imagining
  • Community

The Fellowship focuses on artists who take creative risks to explore, expand, imagine, or re-imagine artistic practices and experiences, and who intentionally and responsibly engage in relationships with audiences and communities.

Early Career Artists

2–10years of generative practice

Fellowship eligibility is the number of years you have been making and presenting your own original work. Age and formal training are not factors.

60 Fellowships Across 7 Artistic Fields

Within every field, Jerome supports a wide range of aesthetics, forms, creative practices, and artistic lineages, and welcomes interdisciplinary and experimental work. Each field has its own panel for application review.

    Key Dates

    The Fellowship application consists of two steps: Step 1: Work Samples & CV (including Eligibility Questionnaire), and Step 2: Core Question Responses.

    Deadlines are in the afternoon, not midnight 2:59 pm Central / 3:59 pm Eastern, so staff can help with technical issues. Late applications aren’t accepted.

    What the Fellowship Supports

    $60Kover three years, $20,000 each year

    Funds are paid directly to you to support your time and expenses. They aren’t tied to a specific project; after an orientation, you’ll design a plan that’s right for your practice.
